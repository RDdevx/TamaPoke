TamaPoke

Firmware: 3.20 · Gen 1–9 (Kanto through Paldea)

Two options: load the game, then load the sprites. Chrome or Edge on a computer, served over HTTPS or localhost.

This browser does not support Web Serial. Use desktop Chrome or Edge.

1Load Tamapoke

Connect the board by USB and press install. This flashes the firmware — the game itself.

Load Tamapoke Browser not supported (use Chrome/Edge). Needs HTTPS or localhost.

Updating? Leave Erase device unchecked to keep your Pokémon and progress. A brand-new board may not show a serial port until you hold BOOT, tap RESET, release BOOT.

2Load Sprites

Insert a microSD card and close the step-1 dialog first. Then press Load Sprites: it connects the board and sends every sprite in the Mon folder of every available region pack to the Mon folder on the SD card (/mons).

Load Sprites

This is the slow part: the sprites travel over the USB serial link (Kanto alone is ~40 MB). Leave the tab open and the card in. Re-running it later is safe — files are simply written again.
